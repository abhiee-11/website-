🏥About Our Hospital

Gunjaman Singh Hospital, founded under Human Development and Community Services (HDCS), operates as a non-profit, no-profit-no-loss institution dedicated to serving marginalized and underserved communities.

📍 Location

Pithuwa, Ward 14, Ratnanagar, Chitwan, Bagmati Province. Serving Chitwan and Makwanpur districts.

🎯 Our Focus

Committed to serving Chepang, Dalit, Janajati, Tharu, and Madhesi communities.

💚 Core Values

Compassion, Accessibility, Excellence, Community Focus, Sustainability, and Transparency.

Hospital Infrastructure

  • 50-bed capacity with modern wards
  • 24/7 electricity and water supply
  • Advanced medical equipment and safety systems
  • Environmental waste management
